Lake Gregory & Waterfront Promenade

Nestled in the heart of Nuwara Eliya, Lake Gregory was created during the British colonial era under Governor Sir William Gregory in 1873. Surrounded by misty hills and alpine pine trees, it is the premier spot for scenic water motor-boating, swans pedal-boating, horse riding along the banks, jet skis, and tranquil sunset walks.

The Ritual of Hill Country High Tea

Since James Taylor first planted commercial tea seeds on Sri Lankan mountain soil in 1867, the Nuwara Eliya region has produced the world’s most prized "Champagne of Teas".

Private Estate Pluckers Trails

Don a traditional woven tea basket and pluck fresh golden buds alongside estate artisans while mist rolls through the terraced slopes.

Grand Hotel Colonial High Tea

Savor oven-warm scones, homemade Nuwara Eliya strawberry preserves, and exquisite pastries on manicured lawns where British governors once dined.

Master Factory Tea Tasting

Learn the 5-step orthodox processing: withering, rolling, fermenting, firing, and grading from BOPF to silver tips with a certified tea taster.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about planning your holiday in Nuwara Eliya and the Central Highlands.

Nuwara Eliya is glorious all year round! The driest and sunniest months are between December and April, which is ideal for hiking World's End and waterfall treks. April is also the famous "Spring Festival" season with horse races, flower shows, and carnival activities. The monsoon months (July to September) bring dramatic romantic mist and roaring waterfalls.

Nuwara Eliya sits at 1,868 meters (over 6,100 feet) above sea level, making it significantly cooler than coastal Sri Lanka. Daytime temperatures hover around 16°C–20°C, and night temperatures can drop to 10°C–12°C. We strongly recommend packing a warm fleece or light jacket, a rain poncho/umbrella, comfortable walking or trekking shoes with good grip, and sunglasses.
